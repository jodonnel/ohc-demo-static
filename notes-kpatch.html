Overview — What Is kpatch

kpatch is live kernel patching. It's included in your RHEL subscription. It applies kernel security fixes in memory — no reboot required. When you do reboot on your normal schedule, the permanent fix is waiting in the updated kernel package.
Emphasize: included in subscription. Not an add-on. Not a third-party tool.
4 / 10

Pros and Limitations

I want to be honest about what kpatch does and doesn't do. It's not a replacement for rebooting — it's a way to extend the window between reboots. The patches are cumulative, so eventually you consolidate with a planned reboot on your schedule. But for critical CVEs — the ones being actively exploited — it gives you immediate protection without downtime.
If someone asks "can we never reboot?" — the answer is no. kpatch buys you time. It does not eliminate reboots.

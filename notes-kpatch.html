Slide 3

What Is kpatch

kpatch is live kernel patching. Included in your RHEL subscription. Applies kernel security fixes in memory -- no reboot required. When you reboot on your normal schedule, the permanent fix is already waiting.
Emphasize: included in subscription. Not an add-on. Not a third-party tool.
Slide 4

Pros and Limitations

kpatch is not a replacement for rebooting -- it extends the window between reboots. Patches are cumulative. Eventually you consolidate with a planned reboot on your schedule. For active CVEs being exploited, it gives immediate protection without downtime.
If asked can we never reboot -- answer is no. kpatch buys time. It does not eliminate reboots.

Executive Brief

How This Demo Works

You scanned a QR code on one of our demo pages. Here's exactly what it does — and what it doesn't.

🍪

No Cookies

This page drops zero cookies. Nothing is stored on your device. Close the tab and it's gone.

👁️

No Tracking

No analytics. No tracking pixels. No fingerprinting. We don't know who you are and we don't want to.

📡

No Third-Party Scripts

No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no ad networks. The only scripts are ours and they're open source.

🔒

Read-Only

The dashboard you're looking at is display-only. It shows events happening on our demo platform in real time. It can't access anything on your device.

📋

What data does this page collect?

None. The demo pages are static HTML served from GitHub Pages. They connect to our Red Hat OpenShift cluster to display live event data using a one-way stream. Your browser receives data. It never sends any.

How it works

1

Edge devices generate events

In the demo, simulated sensors — badge readers, door contacts, HVAC, fire safety — produce structured events called CloudEvents. In production, these would be real building systems.

2

Events flow to Red Hat OpenShift

Each event is sent over an encrypted connection to a managed Kubernetes cluster. The platform classifies, validates, and stores every event.

3

The dashboard shows what's happening now

Your browser opens a one-way connection (Server-Sent Events) and receives a live stream of processed events. That's the counter ticking up, the feed scrolling, and the device map populating — all in real time.

4

The same path leads to SAP

These CloudEvents can flow through SAP Edge Integration Cell into SAP Integration Suite, S/4HANA, and Analytics Cloud. Same events, same platform — the integration is the architecture, not an add-on.
